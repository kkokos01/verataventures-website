# Verata Ventures compact AI brand brief

Use this block at the start of any prompt that creates a Verata Ventures website,
deck, document, memo, proposal, report, email, or campaign asset.

```text
Brand: Verata Ventures
Brand idea: Judgment, accelerated.
Position: Founder-led venture building that combines Fortune-50-caliber executive judgment with AI-native startup velocity. Credible to corporations, venture investors, founders, and individual operators. Never imply fake organizational scale.

Visual system: Live Mineral / Humanist Intelligence. Executive editorial structure plus visible operating metadata. Light mode is the everyday default. Saturated dark mode is reserved for launches, dividers, campaigns, and high-energy moments.

Typography:
- Newsreader 400/500: public editorial headlines, website heroes, presentation covers, section openers, pull quotes.
- Instrument Sans 400/500/600/700: body, navigation, controls, decks, proposals, reports, and every readable part of operational documents.
- Geist Mono 400/500/600: labels, dates, identifiers, status, sources, phase numbers, and page folios only.
- Decision memos use Instrument Sans for titles, values, headings, and body. Never set running copy in mono or serif.

Light colors: background #F3F7F6; surface #FCFEFD; text #071B18; secondary #314A45; muted #5B706B; border #C7D2CF; accessible teal text #007A73; visual teal #00877E; citron #C7F23A; coral #FF5A46.
Dark colors: background #03231F; surface #07342F; raised #0A4039; text #F4FAF8; secondary #B5C8C4; border #35645D; accent #15B8AA; citron #C7F23A; coral #FF5A46.

Color behavior: Light compositions are 80-90% neutral, 5-8% teal, and less than 2% citron plus coral. Citron means positive signal or recommended. Coral means decision, escalation, or outgoing action. Do not use teal, citron, or coral as small text on light backgrounds.

Logo: one-to-many V with one incoming stroke and three parallel outgoing strokes at equal angles and increasing lengths. All four upper terminals are flat and align to one horizontal line. Use the formal ink/mineral mark on light. Use pale/mineral on dark. The pale/mineral/citron/coral expressive mark is dark-mode only. Never fan the outgoing strokes, stagger or angle the top terminals, round, rotate, shadow, glow, gradient, crop, or place the mark in a badge.

Layout: asymmetric but disciplined. 12-column desktop grid, large negative space, short headline line lengths, thin rules, square corners, no default shadows. Lead with one dominant statement rather than equal cards.

Voice: experienced, direct, specific, measured, human. Lead with the conclusion or recommendation. Use evidence, dates, owners, and status. Avoid hype, unsupported superlatives, generic AI language, startup theater, and corporate filler.

Reject: purple gradients; rounded SaaS card grids; centered everything; neon AI imagery; stock handshakes; floating screens; serif memo body; monospaced paragraphs; multicolor mark on light; fake team scale.
```

## Artifact add-ons

### Website

```text
Use a light default experience with one or two dark signal sections. The first viewport should read like a confident editorial poster, not a generic SaaS landing page. Use Newsreader for the major thesis and Instrument Sans everywhere operational. Include proof, status, or source metadata in Geist Mono.
```

### Partner presentation

```text
Use light content slides and dark section dividers. Give each slide one dominant statement, one evidence set, and a mono source or folio. Use the expressive mark only on dark covers or dividers. Avoid dense grids of equal boxes.
```

### Decision memo

```text
Use Instrument Sans for the document title, field values, headings, and body. Use Geist Mono only for TO, FROM, DATE, SUBJECT, memo ID, status, sources, and folio. Use coral for the decision heading and citron for a positive status. Use a light page unless the memo is a screen-only executive card.
```

### Outreach email

```text
Write in Kevin's first-person voice. Lead with the specific reason for reaching out, establish relevance in one sentence, make one direct ask, and keep the default message below 180 words. Use no generic flattery and no oversized logo banner.
```

### Social or campaign asset

```text
Prefer saturated dark mode and the expressive mark. Use one short claim, one proof point, and one clear source or action. Citron and coral may be more visible, but should still behave as signals.
```

## AI validation prompt

```text
Audit the artifact against BRAND.md and brand/tokens.json. Report any deviations in typography role, color value, contrast, logo usage, spacing, audience posture, claims, and voice. Reject generic AI aesthetics, fake scale, serif or mono memo body text, and accent overuse. Do not redesign; identify exact corrections.
```
